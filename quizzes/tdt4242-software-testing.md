[![DevOps](https://img.shields.io/badge/DevOps-0078D7?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DevOps)

A series on core software testing concepts, methods and best practices from the NTNU course TDT4242 Advanced Software Engineering. The course covers methods for developing reliable and complex software systems, including safety-critical and AI-assisted applications:

- **Requirements specification** – turning stakeholder needs into precise technical requirements
- **Test management** – planning and automating modern test processes
- **Code quality** – static analysis, peer review and refactoring
- **Verification and validation** – evaluating large-scale and AI-driven systems
