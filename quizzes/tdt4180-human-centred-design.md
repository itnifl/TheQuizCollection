These quizzes follow the 📖 [CPUX-F](https://uxqb.org/en/cpux-f/) (Certified Professional for Usability and User Experience – Foundation Level) syllabus from UXQB, which is part of TDT4180. The questions come from the public CPUX-F question set and the official CPUX-F Curriculum and Glossary, and together they cover all the Foundation-level learning objectives:

- **Fundamentals and human-centred design** – usability, UX and the ISO 9241-210 process steps (planning, context of use, requirements, design, evaluation)
- **Context of use and user analysis** – users, goals, tasks and environments; personas; user groups; stakeholders; as-is scenarios
- **Requirements engineering** – from user needs to qualitative and quantitative user requirements; human-centred quality objectives
- **Information architecture and interaction design** – navigation structures, dialogue principles (ISO 9241-110), mental models, design patterns, low- and high-fidelity prototypes, storyboards, card sorting
- **Usability evaluation methods** – usability testing (planning, moderation, pre- and post-session interviews), heuristic inspections, surveys, contextual interviews, focus groups, observation, reporting findings
- **UX professional roles and process integration** – responsibilities in planning and managing a human-centred design project, creating deliverables, and iterative refinement through usability evaluation
