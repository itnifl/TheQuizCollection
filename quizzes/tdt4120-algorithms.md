**Content:**
- Efficiency analysis, recursion, divide and conquer
- Ordering, searching, sorting, hashing
- Dynamic programming and greedy methods
- Graph algorithms: shortest paths, matching, spanning trees, maximum flow
- Basics of complexity theory

**Learning outcomes:**
- Recognise and apply a wide range of algorithms
- Analyse time and space costs
- Design new solutions when needed
- Build a foundation for later courses in AI, cyber security and systems
